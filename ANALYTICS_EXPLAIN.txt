Analytics Overview

This section summarizes what each chart and metric in Analytics shows and how it is computed.

1) Demand Forecast (selected medicine)
- Actual usage: Displays the last up-to-15 historical points for the chosen time scale (day/month/year).
- Predicted line (15 future points): Uses a simple linear regression over the last 15 actual points to project future daily usage. Values are clamped to zero or above.
- Reorder panel: 
  • Current Stock: Current totalQuantity for the selected medicine. 
  this is the amount of medicine you physically still have.

your system should compute:
sum of all remaining units from all batches
example:
Batch A → 120 tablets
Batch B → 80 tablets
Current Stock = 200 tablets
this is not boxes.
always tablets/capsules (units).

what it tells the manager:
“how much medicine is left right now?”
but alone, this number is useless.
because 200 tablets could last 2 days… or 2 months.
that’s why the next values exist.

  • Reorder Point: Based on recent usage and a default service-level buffer. 
  this estimates:
how many tablets we will probably sell in the next 30 days
you calculate from past sales (Likely Sales Demand).

formula:
daily usage = total sold in last 30 days ÷ 30
predicted 30-day demand = daily usage × 30

example:
sold last 30 days = 900 tablets
daily usage = 900 ÷ 30 = 30 tablets/day
predicted 30-day demand = 30 × 30 = 900 tablets
now the manager understands consumption speed (Expected Interest).
why this is important

now 200 tablets means:
200 ÷ 30 per day = 6.6 days
so stock is actually almost gone.
without prediction, 200 looks like “a lot”.

  • Predicted 30-day: Sum of the next 30 days of predicted daily usage.
  this is the warning level.

it answers:
at what stock level should i place a new order?
you don’t wait for zero stock.
because suppliers need delivery time.
you add a safety buffer.

formula:
reorder point = daily usage × buffer days
recommended buffer: 7–14 days (use 10 days)

example:
daily usage = 30 tablets/day
buffer = 10 days
reorder point = 30 × 10 = 300 tablets

meaning:
when stock reaches 300 → order now.
not because you’re out,
but because you soon will be.
how they work together
example situation:
Current Stock = 200 tablets
Predicted 30-day demand = 900 tablets
Reorder Point = 300 tablets

interpretation:
you sell 30/day
you only have 200
you will run out in about 6–7 days
supplier might take 5 days to deliver
so the system should show:
REORDER NOW

another case:
Current Stock = 1,500 tablets
Predicted 30-day demand = 900 tablets
Reorder Point = 300 tablets

interpretation:
you have more than 1 month stock.

system shows:
Stock OK
what your system should display to user

example:
Paracetamol 500mg
Current Stock: 200 tablets
Avg usage: 30/day
Predicted demand (30 days): 900 tablets
Reorder Point: 300 tablets
Estimated stock-out: 6 days

Status:
🔴 Reorder recommended
simple understanding
Current Stock → what you have
Predicted Demand → how fast it is used
Reorder Point → when you should buy again
together they prevent:
stock-outs
emergency buying
lost sales
patient inconvenience
this is actually the core purpose of inventory systems.

2) Stock by Category
- Pie distribution of totalQuantity grouped by category.
- Tooltip lists the top medicines (by units) within the hovered category.

3) Total Stock per Medicine
- Shows the top 20 medicines by stock, labeled with name, strength, and dosage form.
- X-axis labels are angled and shortened for readability.

4) Medicines Nearing Expiration
- Bar chart by time bucket: ≤7 days, 8–14, 15–30, and >30 days.
- Also provides a tabular list below with medicine, batch number, and expiry date.

5) Usage Trend (selected medicine)
- When “Day” is chosen, the chart limits to the last 30 daily points to keep it readable.

Assumptions and Notes
- All computations use in-app state and Firebase data (receipts, medicines, batches). 
- Expired batches are excluded from stock totals; FEFO is enforced on sales at the service layer.
